How evidence works. Every claim carries a label. fetched means the agent confirmed it on a live page during the investigation. curated means it was researched by hand and ships with the tool. inferred is the agent's own conclusion from what it found. unverified means nobody confirmed it - check before you rely on it.

Every guide lists what was tested and what stays uncertain. Approval timelines come from provider documentation, not measurement, and providers change their processes - re-run an investigation to refresh a guide.
